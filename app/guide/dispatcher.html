Dispatcher guide

The dispatcher’s board: record what the texts say, see the whole pack at a glance, and share the results. Best on a computer; it works on a phone too.

1. Open the board

In the map’s den menu, pick Dispatcher (the last choice). Or open pack3721.github.io/SFF/?dispatch.

The whole pack’s map is on the left and the board on the right (on a phone, the board is below the map). It’s Week 1 or Week 2 by the date.

2. Two ways to enter texts

Use whichever suits you, or both: quick entry (type a short code) or tabular entry (fill in the grid). They change the same thing.

Quick entry

Click in the box at the top and type a segment code, then Enter:

  • W6 – done
  • W6 5 – done, 5 bags (Week 2)
  • W6 +2 – 2 more bags on a second pass
  • W6! – needs coverage
  • #Wolf B after any of them – the group: W6 5 #Wolf B. Start typing a name and the ones already used pop up; Tab picks the first.
  • W6 u – clear it; commas for several at once: W10 5, W11!, W12

Copied a text from the app? Paste it in; it’s read the same way.

It checks before changing anything. An unknown code is turned down, and if W10 already has bags it asks Replace / Add / Cancel. Undo last entry takes back the last one.

Tabular entry

Under the box, every segment by den, like a spreadsheet: pick its Status, type Bags, 2nd pass and Group right next to the code. Changes save as you leave each cell. Filter by den or status at the top, and hover a row to see when it was entered.

3. The map

Grey until done, then the den’s colour with the bags on the label. Needs coverage with no group yet has the moving white dashes, so it stands out until someone takes it. Click a segment to put its code in the quick entry box and highlight its row.

Sending someone to cover

When W12! comes in, find a group with time, text them, then type W12 #Wolf B. The dashes stop. When they finish, W12 4.

Wolf B adds W12 on their phone with Add by ID on the Mine tab.

4. Groups

Built from the group names you type; nothing to set up. Each group’s den comes from the segments it was given. “2 of ~19” is about how many: the den’s segments less what its other groups have finished, firming up as the den gets done. Segments a group was sent to cover are red. Segments done with no group name sit in Unlabeled rows until you add one.

5. Share and export

  • Share picture: Progress for the pack’s group chat during the walk, Finished for social media. Wide or square. Save picture, or Share… on a phone.
  • Excel: a Dens sheet with totals and a Segments sheet.
  • Backup: everything is saved only in this browser. Save backup now and then, and Restore… it on another computer if you switch.

Tips

  • Use one computer for the whole walk. A backup moves it to another.
  • Texts come in late or out of order; just type them as they arrive. The time saved is when you typed it, not when it was walked.
  • Week 2 bags: the app adds the count to the Done text (“W6 done, 5 bags”), so W6 5 is all you need.
  • On a phone the board sits under the map; drag the map to see the rest.
